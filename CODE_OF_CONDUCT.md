# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
DonClaudio's Lechon House a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming, patient, and respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as addresses or emails)
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
pull requests, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of
Conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, code reviews) and when an individual is officially representing
the project in public.

## Reporting

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers at
**joenelsevellejo831@gmail.com**. Please do not use public issues for conduct reports.
All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the
reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps when deciding consequences:

1. **Correction** - A private, written warning for unprofessional or
   unwelcome behavior, with an explanation of why it was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** - A warning with consequences for continued behavior, including
   no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary Ban** - A temporary ban from any interaction or public
   communication with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent Ban** - A permanent ban from any interaction with the project,
   for a pattern of violations or serious harassment.
